Pre-Ride "ABC" Check for Cargo Bikes

A

Air

Check tire pressure.

B

Brakes

Test front and rear brakes for firm response. Test functioning motor cut-off switches. Inspect pads.

C

Chain, Cranks & Cargo

Ensure the chain is lubricated and clean. Check that the cargo box or rack is bolted securely to the frame.

K

Kids/Cargo Safety

Verify helmets fit snugly and harnesses are buckled.
